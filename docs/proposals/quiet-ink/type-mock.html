Sizes. The three new faces are Japanese fonts; Google only sends the Latin letters to the page, so each adds roughly 20 to 60 KB, not megabytes. All three are under the Open Font License.

Textures here are drawn by the page itself (SVG noise), so they cost almost nothing. A Texturelabs file could replace any of them, as one small image.
