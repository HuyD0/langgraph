Five motion directions

Each one is a live mini lesson. Tap Next step for its step change and Finish for its done moment. Its glyph set is underneath: six phase shapes are the app's own, plus marks for a right answer, a clue, a gate and done.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so the effects jump straight to their end. Turn that off to see them move.

All five follow the app's motion rules: they mark real moments (a step, a finish), never loop and never wait for a tap.

Mixing is fine: for example A's step change with B's finish, or C's glyphs with E's transitions.
